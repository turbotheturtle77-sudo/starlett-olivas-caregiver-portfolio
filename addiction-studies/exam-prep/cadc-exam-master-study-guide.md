## 📝 High-Yield Practice Questions & Explanations

### Question 1 (Ethics & Confidentiality)
**A client's employer calls an addiction counselor requesting verification of attendance in an outpatient program. The client signed a general HIPAA release form. What is the counselor's legal and ethical obligation under 42 CFR Part 2?**
- A) Provide the attendance dates since HIPAA authorization was signed.
- B) Refuse to disclose any information unless a specific written release complying with 42 CFR Part 2 is provided.
- C) Verbally confirm attendance without providing written documentation.
- D) Disclose attendance only if the employer threatens job termination.

> **Correct Answer: B**  
> *Explanation*: Under 42 CFR Part 2, general HIPAA releases do not satisfy federal requirements for substance use disorder (SUD) record disclosures. A specific release naming the recipient, purpose, and expiration date is strictly mandated.

### Question 2 (Counseling Modalities)
**A client states: *"I know I need to stop drinking because it's ruining my health, but alcohol is the only way I can deal with my anxiety."* According to the Transtheoretical Model, which stage of change is the client in?**
- A) Precontemplation
- B) Contemplation
- C) Preparation
- D) Action

> **Correct Answer: B**  
> *Explanation*: Contemplation is characterized by ambivalence—the client recognizes a problem exists and considers change, but experiences conflicting thoughts ("Yes, but...").

### Question 3 (Pharmacology)
**Which neurotransmitter system's sudden rebound hyperactivity is primarily responsible for life-threatening seizures during severe alcohol withdrawal?**
- A) Dopamine
- B) Serotonin
- C) Glutamate
- D) Acetylcholine

> **Correct Answer: C**  
> *Explanation*: Chronic alcohol use suppresses excitatory Glutamate transmission and potentiates inhibitory GABA. Upon abrupt cessation, uninhibited Glutamate surge causes severe autonomic overdrive, seizures, and Delirium Tremens (DTs).

### Question 4 (Assessment & Diagnosis)
**According to DSM-5-TR, how many criteria must a client meet within a 12-month period to receive a diagnosis of Moderate Substance Use Disorder?**
- A) 1 criterion
- B) 2 to 3 criteria
- C) 4 to 5 criteria
- D) 6 or more criteria

> **Correct Answer: C**  
> *Explanation*: Mild = 2–3 criteria; Moderate = 4–5 criteria; Severe = 6+ criteria.

---

### Question 5 (Relapse Prevention)
**What term describes the intense guilt and sense of personal failure experienced after an initial lapse, which can drive a client into full-blown relapse?**
- A) Anhedonia
- B) Abstinence Violation Effect (AVE)
- C) Cognitive Dissonance
- D) Classical Conditioning

> **Correct Answer: B**  
> *Explanation*: Marlatt & Gordon defined the Abstinence Violation Effect (AVE) as the cognitive and emotional reaction to a initial slip/lapse that frequently leads to full relapse if unmitigated.
