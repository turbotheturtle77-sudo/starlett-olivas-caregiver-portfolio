# CADC / CAADE Exam Module 04: Assessment, Diagnosis & DSM-5-TR

High-yield review of clinical screening tools, DSM-5-TR diagnostic criteria, and ASAM Placement Criteria.

---

## 📋 1. DSM-5-TR Substance Use Disorder Criteria

Diagnosis requires meeting **at least 2 out of 11 criteria** within a 12-month period across 4 groupings:

### Impaired Control (1–4)
1. Consuming larger amounts or over a longer period than intended.
2. Persistent desire or unsuccessful efforts to cut down or control use.
3. Spending excessive time obtaining, using, or recovering from the substance.
4. **Craving** or a strong desire/urge to use.

### Social Impairment (5–7)
5. Failure to fulfill major role obligations (work, school, home).
6. Continued use despite persistent social/interpersonal problems.
7. Important social, occupational, or recreational activities given up or reduced.

### Risky Use (8–9)
8. Recurrent use in physically hazardous situations (e.g., driving impaired).
9. Continued use despite knowledge of persistent physical or psychological problems caused/exacerbated by use.

### Pharmacological Criteria (10–11)
10. **Tolerance** (increased amount needed, or diminished effect).
11. **Withdrawal** (characteristic syndrome, or substance taken to relieve/avoid symptoms).

---

## 📊 Severity Classification

- **Mild**: Meets 2 to 3 criteria.
- **Moderate**: Meets 4 to 5 criteria.
- **Severe**: Meets 6 or more criteria.

## 🛠️ 2. Common Clinical Screening & Assessment Instruments

| Instrument | Full Name | Application / Target |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **CAGE** | Cut down, Annoyed, Guilty, Eye-opener | Quick 4-question alcohol screening tool (2+ indicates positive). |
| **AUDIT** | Alcohol Use Disorders Identification Test | 10-item WHO screening tool measuring consumption, behaviors, and consequences. |
| **DAST-10** | Drug Abuse Screening Test | 10-item screening tool for non-alcohol drug use. |
| **CIWA-Ar** | Clinical Institute Withdrawal Assessment for Alcohol | Assessment tool for monitoring alcohol withdrawal severity and dosing medication. |
| **COWS** | Clinical Opiate Withdrawal Scale | 11-item scale to assess opioid withdrawal severity (determines when to initiate Buprenorphine). |
| **ASI** | Addiction Severity Index | Comprehensive semi-structured interview covering 7 domain areas. |

## 📐 3. ASAM Patient Placement Criteria (6 Dimensions)

Determines appropriate level of care (Outpatient, Intensive Outpatient, Residential, Inpatient Medically Managed).

1. **Dimension 1**: Acute Intoxication and/or Withdrawal Potential.
2. **Dimension 2**: Biomedical Conditions and Complications.
3. **Dimension 3**: Emotional, Behavioral, or Cognitive Conditions and Complications.
4. **Dimension 4**: Readiness to Change.
5. **Dimension 5**: Relapse, Continued Use, or Continued Problem Potential.
6. **Dimension 6**: Recovery Environment.
