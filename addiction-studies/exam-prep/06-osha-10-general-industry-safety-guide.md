# OSHA 10 General Industry & Operational Safety Study Guide

High-yield study material covering **OSHA 10-Hour Certification** fundamentals, essential for union operations (Local 47), logistics (UPS), and caretaking/healthcare environments.

---

## 🛡️ 1. Introduction to OSHA & Worker Rights

- **OSHA Mission**: Enacted in 1970 to assure safe and healthful working conditions for workers by setting and enforcing standards.
- **Worker Rights Under OSHA**:
  - Right to a safe workplace free from recognized serious hazards (General Duty Clause — Section 5(a)(1)).
  - Right to request OSHA inspection without fear of retaliation (Whistleblower Protection — Section 11(c)).
  - Right to access medical and exposure records.
  - Right to receive training in a language the worker understands.

---

## ⚡ 2. Electrical Safety & Lockout/Tagout (LOTO)

- **BE SAFE Hazards**: **B**urns, **E**lectrocution, **S**hock, **A**rc Flash/Arc Blast, **F**ire, **E**xplosions.
- **Ground Fault Circuit Interrupters (GFCI)**: Fast-acting circuit breakers designed to shut off electric power in 1/40th of a second when a ground fault is detected.
- **Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) — 29 CFR 1910.147**:
  - Disabling machinery/equipment to prevent unexpected energization or release of stored energy during maintenance.
  - **Only authorized employees** who applied the lock/tag are permitted to remove it.

---

## ⚠️ 3. Hazard Communication (HazCom) & SDS

- **OSHA Standard**: 29 CFR 1910.1200 (Aligned with GHS — Globally Harmonized System).
- **Safety Data Sheets (SDS)**: Mandatory 16-section standardized documents providing chemical hazard info, handling, storage, and emergency measures.
- **Chemical Label Requirements**: Product Identifier, Signal Word (*DANGER* for severe hazards, *WARNING* for less severe), Hazard Statements, Precautionary Statements, GHS Pictograms, Manufacturer Info.

---

## 🪜 4. Walking-Working Surfaces & Fall Protection

- **Trigger Height for Fall Protection (General Industry)**: **4 Feet** above lower levels (29 CFR 1910.28).
- **Guardrail System Standards**:
  - Top rail height: 42 inches (± 3 inches).
  - Midrail height: 21 inches.
  - Toeboards: Minimum 3.5 inches high to prevent falling tools.
- **Personal Fall Arrest System (PFAS)**: Anchorage (5,000 lbs rating per worker), Body Harness, Lanyard/Lifeline.

---

## 🦺 5. Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)

- **Employer Responsibility**: Employers **must provide PPE at no cost** to workers (with rare exceptions like non-specialized safety-toe footwear).
- **Hierarchy of Controls** (Most effective to least effective):
  1. **Elimination** (Remove hazard)
  2. **Substitution** (Replace hazard)
  3. **Engineering Controls** (Isolate hazard, e.g., ventilation, guardrails)
  4. **Administrative Controls** (Change work methods, training, rotations)
  5. **PPE** (Protect worker with barrier — Last line of defense)
