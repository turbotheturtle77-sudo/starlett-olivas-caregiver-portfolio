# CADC / CAADE Exam Module 03: Ethics, Legal Standards & 42 CFR Part 2

Essential legal and ethical standards for addiction counselors and behavioral health professionals.

---

## 🔒 1. 42 CFR Part 2 vs. HIPAA

Substance Use Disorder (SUD) treatment records carry **stricter federal privacy protections** than standard medical records under HIPAA.

| Rule / Regulation | Scope & Authority | Primary Requirement |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **42 CFR Part 2** | Federal regulation governing federally assisted SUD programs. | **Strict Written Consent Required**: Prohibits disclosure of SUD patient records/identity without specific written consent, even to general healthcare providers, except in narrow emergency exceptions. |
| **HIPAA** | General Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act. | Governs protected health information (PHI) across healthcare entities; permits disclosure for Treatment, Payment, and Operations (TPO) without consent. |

### Key Takeaway for CADC Exam:
*42 CFR Part 2 consent forms MUST explicitly name the recipient, purpose, amount of information disclosed, and expiration date. General releases do NOT satisfy 42 CFR Part 2.*

---

## ⚖️ 2. Exceptions to Confidentiality (Mandated Disclosure)

Confidentiality **MUST** be broken under specific legal mandates:

1. **Child Abuse / Neglect**: Mandatory reporting to Child Protective Services (CPS) or law enforcement.
2. **Elder / Dependent Adult Abuse**: Mandatory reporting to Adult Protective Services (APS).
3. **Imminent Danger to Self or Others (Duty to Warn / Protect — Tarasoff Rule)**: Reasonable effort to notify intended victim and law enforcement when a client presents a serious threat of physical violence.
4. **Medical Emergency**: Information disclosed strictly to medical personnel for treating an immediate life-threatening emergency.
5. **Valid Court Order**: Subpoenas alone are **NOT** sufficient under 42 CFR Part 2. A specific court order signed by a judge satisfying Part 2 requirements is mandatory.

---

## 🛑 3. Professional Boundaries & Ethics

- **Dual Relationships**: Counselors must avoid multiple roles with clients (e.g., counseling a friend, family member, business partner, or employee).
- **Sexual Contact**: Strictly prohibited with current clients and former clients (CAADE guidelines require a minimum multi-year ban; zero-tolerance for active clients).
- **Scope of Practice**: Practicing strictly within the boundaries of CADC certification. Diagnosing mental health disorders outside SUD requires appropriate licensed supervision (LCSW, LMFT, PsyD).
- **Self-Care & Impairment**: Ethical duty to seek professional help and refrain from counseling if personal substance use or emotional impairment compromises client care.
