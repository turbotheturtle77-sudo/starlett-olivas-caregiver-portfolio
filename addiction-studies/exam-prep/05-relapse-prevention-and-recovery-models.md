# CADC / CAADE Exam Module 05: Relapse Prevention & Recovery Models

High-yield study material covering evidence-based relapse prevention frameworks and recovery support modalities.

---

## ⚡ 1. Relapse Prevention Frameworks

### Terence Gorski’s Relapse Prevention Model (CENAPS)
- **Core Premise**: Relapse is a *process*, not an isolated event. Substance use is the final step in a chain of warning signs.
- **Phases of Relapse**:
  1. **Emotional Relapse**: Not thinking about using, but emotional/behavioral responses set the stage (isolation, poor self-care, bottled emotions).
  2. **Mental Relapse**: Internal war between wanting to use and wanting to stay sober (craving, bargaining, minimizing consequences, planning use).
  3. **Physical Relapse**: The act of acquiring and consuming the substance.

### Marlatt & Gordon Cognitive-Behavioral Model
- Focuses on **High-Risk Situations (HRS)** and coping responses.
- **Abstinence Violation Effect (AVE)**: The guilt, shame, and perceived loss of control experienced after an initial slip/lapse, which often accelerates a minor lapse into a full-blown relapse if unmanaged.

## 🤝 2. Support Modalities & Recovery Frameworks

### A. 12-Step Mutual Aid Groups (AA, NA, Al-Anon)
- **Philosophy**: Disease model of addiction; spiritual/peer-support framework focusing on surrender, self-examination, restitution, and service.
- **Role of Counselor**: Support client engagement with mutual aid; reframe misconceptions; respect client autonomy.

### B. SMART Recovery (Self-Management and Recovery Training)
- **Philosophy**: Science-based 4-point program based on REBT (Rational Emotive Behavior Therapy) and CBT. Does not use labels like "addict" or "alcoholic."

### C. Medication-Assisted Treatment (MAT)
FDA-approved medications combined with counseling and behavioral therapies:

- **Opioid Use Disorder (OUD)**:
  - **Methadone**: Full mu-opioid agonist (administered in accredited OTP clinics).
  - **Buprenorphine (Suboxone)**: Partial mu-opioid agonist with Naloxone ceiling effect.
  - **Naltrexone (Vivitrol)**: Full opioid antagonist (requires 7–10 days opioid-free before initiation).
- **Alcohol Use Disorder (AUD)**:
  - **Disulfiram (Antabuse)**: Aldehyde dehydrogenase inhibitor (causes severe flushing/nausea if alcohol is consumed).
  - **Acamprosate (Campral)**: Restores GABA/Glutamate balance.
  - **Naltrexone**: Reduces alcohol cravings and euphoric reward effects.
