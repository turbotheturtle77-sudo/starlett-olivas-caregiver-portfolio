# Addiction Studies & Certification Roadmap

Overview of academic coursework at **East Los Angeles Community College** and preparation for state/national licensure.

---

## 🎓 Academic Program Details
- **Institution**: East Los Angeles Community College (ELAC)
- **Degree Program**: Associate of Arts (A.A.) in Addiction Studies
- **Expected Graduation**: Fall 2028

---

## 📚 Key Curriculum & Skill Competencies

| Course Module | Skill Focus |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Substance Use Disorders** | Understanding etiology, progression, and diagnostic frameworks for addiction. |
| **Counseling Techniques** | Individual and group counseling methodology, motivational interviewing. |
| **Recovery Support** | Relapse prevention strategies, 12-step integration, community support networks. |
| **Pharmacology of Addiction** | Physiological and neurological impacts of psychoactive substances. |
| **Ethics in Counseling** | Legal standards, professional boundaries, confidentiality, and client rights. |

---

## 📜 Licensure & Credentialing Track

- **CAADE / CADC Certification**: Actively completing academic course units and practical hours required for California Association for Alcohol/Drug Educators (CAADE) and California Association of DUI Treatment Programs / Certified Alcohol and Drug Counselor (CADC) credentialing.
