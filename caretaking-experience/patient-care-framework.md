# Patient Care & Caregiving Experience Framework

Documented methodology and clinical care experience of **Starlett Olivas** (Visiting Angels & Private Home Care, 2019–2021).

---

## 🛡️ Core Caretaking Pillar & Practices

### 1. Direct Patient Care & ADL Assistance
- **Activities of Daily Living (ADLs)**: Assisting clients with hygiene, mobility, nutrition, and comfort while maintaining maximum patient dignity and independence.
- **Medication Adherence**: Monitoring schedules and ensuring prescribed medication compliance in strict coordination with healthcare providers and family care plans.

### 2. Behavioral Monitoring & Documentation
- **Observation Protocols**: Recording physical health indicators, cognitive fluctuations, and emotional state changes.
- **Incident Reporting**: Promptly communicating status shifts to primary physicians, nurse supervisors, and designated family advocates.

### 3. Patient Privacy & HIPAA Compliance
- Safeguarding confidential patient information and care records.
- Maintaining secure, ethical boundaries within home environments and care facilities.

### 4. Crisis Management & De-escalation
- Utilizing active listening, non-violent communication, and calm presence during emotional distress or agitation.
- Bridging caretaking experience directly into future addiction counseling practice.
